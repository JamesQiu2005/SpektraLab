# The Windows pages

Written by `design/src/gen_windows.py`; do not edit by hand. [README.md](README.md) is the specification (the SpektraLab HIG for Windows).

Sizes are effective pixels (epx). The editor and the Library are drawn in the window of 1920 × 1080 at 125 %, maximised above the taskbar: 1536 × 816 (a 32 epx title bar, then 784 of content). PC-N1 and PC-N2 are the right half of that screen, snapped (768 × 816); PC-N3 is 1920 × 1080 at 150 %, maximised (1280 × 672); Settings is its own window, 860 × 600.

The ids start `PC-` so they never meet the iPad's `W-` ids. The last column is the Mac page each one answers (`../mac/INDEX.md`); — marks a page the Mac set does not have.
